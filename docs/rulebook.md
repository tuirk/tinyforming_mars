# TINYforming Mars — Complete Rulebook

**By Michael Bevilacqua**  
**1–2 Players**

---

## Overview

TINYforming Mars is a 1- to 2-player game set in the distant future where mankind has mastered the technology needed to turn lifeless-neighbor Mars into a thriving destination for humanity.

---

## Components

1. Project Cards ×14 (double-sided)
2. Map Cards ×3 (Tharsis, Elysium, and one additional)
3. Credits ×10
4. Player Tokens: White ×3, Black ×3 (2 City Tokens and 1 Special Project Token per player)
5. Parameter Tiles: Heat (Red) ×11, Greenery (Green) ×7, Water (Blue) ×4
6. Resource Tokens: Nature ×2, Production ×1, Science ×1

---

## Objective

Each player is attempting to terraform Mars by completing projects that fulfill one or more of Mars' parameters for sustaining life: Heat, Oxygen (provided by Greenery), and Water. The player that is able to complete more projects towards this goal, while building cities on prime Martian real estate, will be the winner.

---

## Game Flow

The game takes place over several rounds, known as **Generations**, in which players will alternate taking turns. First the players will draft Project Cards which describe Projects that they have access to. Then, on a player's turn, they can complete one of their own Project Cards, one of the Standard Projects, or pass. When both players have passed, the Generation is over and players collect income to begin the next Generation.

---

## End Game

The three parameters: Heat, Greenery, and Water are represented by Red, Green, and Blue Tiles, respectively. The players will acquire or place these Tiles by completing Projects and Standard Projects. When the supply of at least 2 of the 3 types have been exhausted **OR** there are no unoccupied hexes on the map **OR** Generation 12 has been reached, whichever comes first, that Generation will be the final Generation of the game. Once that Generation is over, the players will count their scores. Highest score wins.

---

## Setup

1. Players decide which Mars Map they want to use and set it up in the center of the table.

2. Players will randomly determine who will be the White player and who will be the Black player. The White player starts the game and will be first in the odd Generations (rounds 1,3,5…) and the Black player will be first in the even Generations (rounds 2,4,6…). Each player takes the 3 Player Tokens that match the color they are playing.

3. Each player takes 5 Credits.

4. The 3 types of Parameter Tiles (Red, Green, and Blue) are placed off to the side of the Map to create a supply.

5. The 4 Resource Tokens (Nature ×2, Production, and Science) are placed off to the side of the Map to create a supply.

6. Take the 14 Project Cards and shuffle them. Place them at the side of the board to create a Draw Deck, with room for a discard pile next to it. Reshuffle discard pile to create new deck as needed during game.

7. The White player draws and flips the first Project Card face-up and determines which end will face them and which will face the Black player. The Black player draws and flips the second Project Card face-up and determines which end will face them and which will face the White player. Finally, the White player draws and flips the third Project Card face-up and determines which end will face them and which will face the Black player.

8. The Black player places one of their Black City Tokens in any hex not reserved for water. This is the starting location of their first city. The White player then places one of their White City Tokens in any hex not reserved for water *and not adjacent to the hex occupied by the Black player's city*. This is the starting location of their first city.

---

## Parameters

The three Parameters, Heat, Greenery, and Water are how you terraform Mars. They are represented by Red, Green, and Blue Tiles, respectively. They are acquired by activating Card Projects and Standard Projects. **When at least 2 out of the 3 Parameter Tile types have been exhausted from the supply, the end of game has been triggered and that will be the final Generation.**

### Heat

When a player gains a Heat Tile, they take a Red Tile from the supply and add it to their own personal supply. Each Heat Tile in a player's personal supply is worth 1 victory point at the end of the game. A Heat Tile on the map is worth −1 point to any city adjacent to that Heat Hex.

### Greenery

When placing a Greenery Tile, take a Green Tile from the supply and add it to an unoccupied land hex on the map (one not reserved for water). Each Greenery Tile adjacent to a city scores that city's owner 1 victory point — **plus** 1 additional VP if it is adjacent to only that player's city during end game scoring.

### Water

When placing a Water Tile, take a Blue Tile from the supply and add it to an unoccupied Water Hex on the map. A hex that is reserved for water has a light blue background. When placing a Water Tile, if the hex has a Resource Token icon on it, gain a matching Resource Token from the supply, if available. **In addition, if the Water Tile is placed adjacent to any other Water Tiles, the player who placed it gains 1 Credit for each adjacent Water Tile.** During the Income Phase of a Generation, a Water Tile adds 1 to the income of each adjacent city. Each Water Tile that is adjacent to only one of the two players' city(ies) is worth 1 victory point to that player at the end of the game.

---

## Map

Each map of Mars is made up of 19 hexes. 14 of those hexes can have Greenery Tiles or City Tokens placed on them, and 5 blue-tinted hexes are reserved for Water Tiles.

### Hex Types

**Bonus Hex:** A Bonus Hex has a Tag in the center and a thick border matching the color of the Tag. If a player has a city in a Bonus Hex, it counts as having one of that Tag for as long as their city is on that Bonus Hex. There are 5 Bonus Hexes per map.

**Empty Hex:** There are 9 Empty Hexes that offer no additional benefit.

**Water Hex:** A Water Hex has a light blue background. If a player places a Parameter Tile in a Water Hex containing a Resource Token icon, they immediately gain that token to their supply.

### Tharsis Map — Bonus Hexes

- Production (orange border, factory icon) — top-left area
- Production (orange border, factory icon) — bottom-left area
- Nature (green border, plant icon) — far-left, middle row
- Nature (green border, plant icon) — far-right, middle row
- Space (grey border, rocket icon) — bottom-right area

### Elysium Map — Bonus Hexes

- Science (white border, lightbulb icon) — right side, upper area
- Space (grey border, rocket icon) — left side, upper area
- Nature (green border, plant icon) — center, middle row
- Production (orange border, factory icon) — bottom-left
- Production (orange border, factory icon) — bottom-right

## Project Cards

On a player's turn, if they meet all the requirements of one of their Project Cards (the projects facing that player), they can activate that Project in order to perform that card's ability. These abilities usually allow the player to gain a Heat Tile or place a Greenery or Water Tile on the Map. The effective use of Project Cards will help win the game. A player can only activate each of their Project Cards once per Generation. Remember you've used it by marking it with your blank Player Token.

### Card Anatomy

1. **Project Name**

2. **Cost in Credits:** If the Cost has an * after the number, the cost can be reduced by an effect of the Project. When paying for a Project, the player must place 1 Credit on the Project Card to show they have used that Project this Generation. Additional Credits used to pay the cost are returned to the supply.

3. **Requirements:** Beside the credit cost, each Project requires that you have access to a certain quantity and type of Tag. The Tags can come from any combination of: your Project Cards (see #6), your cities on bonus hexes, and from spending (discarding) Resource Tokens. In addition to the Tags, some Projects also require a certain number of Parameter Tiles. The Heat requirement refers to the **combined** amount of both players' personal supplies of Heat Tiles *plus* any Heat Tiles on the map. The Greenery or Water Tile requirement refers to Green/Blue Tiles on the Map.

4. **Effect Icons:** The ability of the project is depicted here for quick reference. Also, the background color of the card indicates which of the three parameters the project will help complete. Red/Heat, Green/Greenery, and Blue/Water. There is also Grey; this type will not complete parameters, but will offer some other ability.

5. **Effect:** The ability of the Project Card is described in words to correspond with the Effect Icons. If there are no available Parameter Tiles that match the corresponding Project, that Project cannot be activated. **IMPORTANT: The text of a Project Card supersedes this rulebook.**

6. **Tags:** Each Project Card immediately offers 2 Tags on the side facing you that can be used to meet the requirements of any of your Project Cards, or of a Standard Project. You do not have to complete the Project to gain the Tags the Project provides, this is automatic and you have them as soon as the cards are chosen at the beginning of each Generation.

### The 5 Tag Types

- **Energy** (lightning bolt icon)
- **Production** (factory icon)
- **Nature** (leaf/plant icon)
- **Science** (lightbulb icon)
- **Space** (rocket icon)

## Standard Projects

Once per Generation, each player may complete one of the five Standard Projects shown on the Map card if its requirements are met. When you activate a Standard Project, place your blank Player Token on the Standard Project spot as a reminder that *another* one cannot be activated this Generation. Most of the Standard Projects have a cost and Tag requirement similar to Project Cards.

1. **Sell Patent:** Gain 1 Credit. There is no requirement/cost for this. If there are no Credits in the supply, this action cannot be taken.

2. **Build City:** Cost 2 Credits. Requires: Production, Space tags. Place or relocate one Player City Token on the map, observing these rules: **a)** A player is limited to 2 cities. **b)** A city must be placed in an unoccupied hex on the map. **c)** Players cannot place a city in a hex reserved for water (light blue background). **d)** Players cannot place a city adjacent to any other city. If a player has already placed their cities, they may take this Standard Project to relocate one of their cities to a new hex, observing all the same placement rules.

3. **Import Water:** Cost 3 Credits. Requires: Science, Space tags. Place an available Water Tile from the supply onto an unoccupied hex reserved for water.

4. **Greenhouses:** Cost 3 Credits. Requires: Production, Nature, Nature tags. Place an available Greenery Tile from the supply onto an unoccupied hex not reserved for water.

5. **Energy Farms:** Cost 3 Credits. Requires: Energy, Science tags. The player gains an available Heat Tile from the supply.

---

## Resource Tokens

When a Parameter Tile is placed in a space reserved for a Water Tile that has a Resource Token icon printed on it, that player gains a Token matching that icon from the supply. If there are no matching Resource Tokens available, the player gains nothing. A Resource Token can be spent *by returning it to the supply* when a player needs that matching Tag for a Project or Standard Project.

Some Projects offer the activating player their choice of an available Resource Token. When this ability is activated, the Resource Token comes from the supply. The three types of Resource Tokens are Nature (×2), Production, and Science.

## Generations (Gameplay)

The White Player begins odd Generations and the Black Player begins even Generations. Each Generation (round) the following phases happen in this order:

1. **Research Phase**
2. **Action Phase**
3. **Income Phase**

### Phase 1: Research Phase

The first phase of each Generation is the Research Phase. During this phase, the players will draw the Project Cards that they will have access to that Generation (if you have gone through Setup, the first Generation's cards have already been drawn). Starting with the Generation's start player, take a Project Card from the draw pile, flip it face-up, and decide which Project will face you and which will face your opponent. The second player repeats this process for the second Project Card. The starting player then repeats this process for the third and final Project Card. At the end of the Research Phase, each player will have access to only the 3 Projects that are facing them.

### Phase 2: Action Phase

The Generation's start player will either complete one of their Project Cards, complete a Standard Project, or Pass. After they take one action, the second player will take one action. Actions will be taken, one at a time, between each player, until one player passes. After a player passes, they can no longer take any actions during that action phase. The player who has not passed can continue taking actions until they pass.

In order to complete a Project or a Standard Project, the player must have the required Tags, Credits, and meet any Parameters listed. If a player cannot meet all the requirements, they cannot activate the Project or Standard Project. *Note: Players have access to the Tags provided on the bottom of their Project Cards whether the Project is activated or not.*

**Reminders:**
- A player may never complete the same Project twice in the same Generation.
- A player may only complete 1 Standard Project per Generation.
- If a player should gain Credits because of an action and there are not enough available from the supply, they receive what is in the supply and the rest are lost.
- Credits placed on Projects cannot be removed during the Action Phase.

When both players have passed, all Credits on Projects are returned to the supply. The Project Cards for that Generation are discarded (shuffle the discard pile when the draw pile is empty to create a new draw pile).

### Phase 3: Income Phase

Starting with the Generation's start player, they will collect **1 Credit for each of their cities.** They will also collect **1 Credit for each Water Tile adjacent to each of their cities.** A Water Tile touching both of a player's cities counts for each of those cities.

After the start player has added their Income Phase Credits to any Credits they did not use during the Action Phase, they must **return to the supply all Credits above 5** (Martian taxes are severe). The second player collects income following the same rules.

**Check for End Game:** If the supply of Parameter Tiles have not been exhausted from at least 2 of the 3 types and there is at least one unoccupied hex on the map and Generation 12 has not been reached, the next Generation begins and the start player will shift to the player who was not the start player during the current Generation.

If the supply of Parameter Tiles has been exhausted from at least 2 of the 3 types **OR** there are no more unoccupied hexes **OR** Generation 12 has been reached, the game is over and final scoring takes place.

---

## End Game Scoring

Once the last Generation has completed, the players will calculate their scores:

### Each City scores:
- +1 point for each adjacent Greenery Tile
- −1 point for each adjacent Heat Tile (on the map)

### Greenery & Water Tiles each score:
- +1 Point to the owner of a city adjacent to it, if it is only adjacent to one of the players' cities, not both.

### Heat Tiles score:
- +1 point for each Heat Tile in the player's personal supply.

### Tiebreaker

If players tie, compare the following until one player has more than the other in this order:

1. Points from Cities
2. Points from Greenery Tiles
3. Points from Water Tiles
4. Points from Heat Tiles
